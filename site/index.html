LATEST PATCH NOTES

  1. 2026-10-26 NEXT LEVEL

    A real song, and a game that follows it

    The soundtrack is now Press Start, made with Suno. Faster-whisper timed every lyric line (339 of 352 words matched), and the game re-times all five stages to whichever song is loaded, so a new song drops in without hand-timing.

  2. 2026-10-26 NEXT LEVEL

    Full CI/CD to my own server

    Every push is type-checked, built and played through by a headless test suite, then packaged as a read-only, unprivileged Docker image and smoke-tested. Merges to main deploy it to a VPS behind Cloudflare, with health checks, automatic rollback and a check that the live site serves the new commit.

  3. 2026-10-26 NEXT LEVEL

    Secrets, transitions and phone controls

    Five hidden secrets with a fireworks ending for finding them all, four new stage transitions (a pixel upgrade, a CRT power-off, a race that shrinks into a phone, a VR world that opens out of it), and thumb pads beside the screen on phones.

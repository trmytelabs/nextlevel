LATEST PATCH NOTES

  1. 2026-10-24 NEXT LEVEL

    Finale rebuilt as a VR saber lane with an AI co-pilot

    The last era was agents building a neon city ahead of a runner. It did not land, so it is now two sabers, word blocks on the beat, and a co-pilot that turns the words you cut into arches, pillars and rings ahead of you. The sabers follow your mouse or finger into the lane, and the headset-on transition at bar 75 is the moment the genre changes.

  2. 2026-10-24 NEXT LEVEL

    Racer: one gantry per lyric line, not a word every half second

    Streaming individual words past the car was unreadable. Each line is now a single overhead gantry every two bars with the racing line lit on one lane; drive under it in that lane to take the line. Track widened, full-resolution antialiased render, and CC0 textures for asphalt, grass and walls. Fixed an inverted banking sign while in there.

  3. 2026-10-24 NEXT LEVEL

    Platformer made Mario-proper, plus a headless test harness

    The 16-bit era now runs on the ground with pits and pipes; plain bricks shatter from below and ? blocks pop a phrase of the lyric. Behind it, a puppeteer-core harness drives the full three minutes in headless Edge, checks era boundaries and page errors, and runs on every push. Server side: a Caddy config with HTTPS and security headers, a restricted rsync deploy user, and a deploy workflow gated on the tests passing.

**How to play**
- Watch: it plays itself. Press a movement key or touch the screen to take over; it picks back up after two idle seconds.
- Keyboard: WASD or arrows to move, W or up to jump, space to fire. In the finale, your mouse moves the sabers.
- Phone: turn it sideways. Hold the left or right edge to move, tap the top half to jump, the bottom half to fire. In the finale, your finger moves the sabers.
